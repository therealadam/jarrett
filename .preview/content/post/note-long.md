Been thinking about how the best tools disappear when you're using them. A good pen, a sharp knife, a text editor you've configured for a decade. You stop noticing the tool and start noticing the work. The worst tools are the ones that keep asking for your attention, reminding you they exist, wanting to be updated or synced or signed into. I want more of the former and fewer of the latter.
